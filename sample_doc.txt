ACME Corporation Employee Handbook — 2024 Edition

SECTION 1: WORKING HOURS
Standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM local time.
Employees are entitled to a one-hour unpaid lunch break each day.
Flexible working arrangements must be approved in writing by the employee's direct manager.
Remote work is permitted up to three days per week for eligible roles.

SECTION 2: LEAVE POLICY
Annual Leave: Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Sick Leave: Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences exceeding three consecutive days.
Parental Leave: Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave.
Bereavement Leave: Up to 5 days of paid leave for the death of an immediate family member.

SECTION 3: CODE OF CONDUCT
Employees must treat all colleagues, clients, and vendors with respect and professionalism.
Harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind will not be tolerated and may result in immediate termination.
Confidential company information must not be shared outside the organisation without written approval.
Personal use of company equipment for illegal activities is strictly prohibited.

SECTION 4: COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS
Salaries are reviewed annually in January.
Employees are eligible for a performance bonus of up to 15% of annual base salary, awarded in February.
The company provides private health insurance for all full-time employees and their immediate dependants.
A company pension contribution of 5% of gross salary is matched up to 5% by the employer.

SECTION 5: TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT
Each employee has an annual learning budget of $1,500 for external courses, conferences, or certifications.
Internal mentorship programmes are available and can be arranged through HR.
All new employees must complete mandatory compliance training within the first 30 days of employment.

SECTION 6: IT AND SECURITY
Employees must not install unauthorised software on company devices.
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and changed every 90 days.
Any suspected security breach must be reported to the IT Security team within one hour of discovery.
Company data must not be stored on personal devices or unapproved cloud services.

SECTION 7: GRIEVANCE PROCEDURE
Employees who have a workplace grievance should first attempt to resolve it informally with their manager.
If unresolved, a formal grievance can be submitted in writing to the HR department.
HR will acknowledge the grievance within 5 business days and aim to resolve it within 30 business days.
Employees may be accompanied by a colleague or trade union representative during formal grievance meetings.
